## Rabbit holes
1. **The Google interview loop:** DS&A coding rounds plus a system design round at Google scale. This is the real prep, more than any single keyword.
2. **Android and Kotlin basics:** enough to talk about Play's client side; Java transfers fast.
3. **Google Play surfaces:** store, Play Console, Play Billing, Play Protect, Play Games. Know which team you'd want.
4. **Large-scale system design:** sharding, caching, replication, rate limiting at 100K+ QPS.
